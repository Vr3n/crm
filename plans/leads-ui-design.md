# Lead Management UI Planning.

Use the skills `design-taste-frontend`, `high-end-visual-design` and other ui related skills.

Read the modules `01-people-leads-sales.md` and `02-customer-and-memberships.md` to understand the context of lead management.
After understanding the context and business requirement you should research the internet thoroughly for best lead management Ui and UX flows.

getting the UI and UX is the most important for our application.

The staff is not tech savvy. They should be able to easily add or modify leads, See history, and analyze the leads.
Also research about how the lead tracking should be shown in lead detail page, The lead detail page should follow bento layout.
Tables should be sleek and minimal like air-tables and design language should be thoroughly implemented. It should speak more by showing less and not overwhelming the staff.

Then research the Metrics to show in the leads page. It should show how many leads are present in each stage. and also how many leads we have aquired.
It should be configurable with filters and date range so the owner can also export reports if they want.
