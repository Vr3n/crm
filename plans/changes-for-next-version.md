# New Features.

Client wants these features for new version.

## Lead / Customer Blacklisting

The Customer / Lead can be black listed. Front desk can Give Review of the Customer / Lead, and mark them as black listed. \
For e.g a Customer is too loud in gym, creates rukus and also is rude to everyone, doesn't respect the equipments. \
Gym owner can decide to blacklist the customer, so whenever the customer tries to renew or contact, front desk can see that the customer / lead is blacklisted and they don't have to renew the membership. \

Research about the usecase, how other CRMs or Softwares handle this type of usecase.

## Leaad Status

Add new status for leads: `Do Not Disturb`, `Not Interested`

## Lead / Customer Photo.

Frontdesk can add a photograph of Lead / Customer. So they can be identified easily.

## Plans

The plans should have an start and end date.
Default value of start date should be today. and end date should be open-ended.
